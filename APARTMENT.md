# Apartment identity

The fictional apartment is on the second floor of an ordinary early-20th-century tenement near Nowy Kleparz in Kraków. Its quiet internal courtyard wraps around the north and east façades. The rooms are modest, partly renovated, and lived in. The palette combines aged oak, muted green upholstery, cream joinery, off-white plaster, and restrained textiles.

## Coordinates and layout

One unit is one metre. The northwestern outer corner is (0, 0). Plan x grows east and y grows south. Heights are measured above the floor. The model spans 8.20 × 8.40 m, with a 3.10 m ceiling. Plan y is Three.js z, and height is Three.js y.

| Area | Design dimensions and implemented geometry |
| --- | --- |
| West and east bedrooms | Nominal 3.40 × 5.20 m each. West x 0–3.40 and east x 4.80–8.20, both y 0–5.20 before accounting for wall thickness. Doors face each other at y 3.40–4.30 and both windows face north. |
| Corridor | 1.40 m is the nominal wall-line dimension from x 3.40 to 4.80. The implemented clear span between opposing wall faces is 1.16 m (x 3.52–4.68). |
| Storage and laundry | Nominal 1.40 × 1.65 m at the north end. The clear span is approx. 1.16 × 1.51 m before the doorway lintel and wall at y 1.65. Single corridor entrance. The existing washing machine and shelves remain. |
| Bathroom | Southwest, behind the wall at y 5.20. Its corridor door spans y 6.45–7.35. Shower, basin, mirror, WC and towel storage remain in place. |
| Kitchen | Southeast and open to the corridor. Worktop against the bedroom partition, dining area farther south, and east-facing opening at y 6.10–7.65. |
| Entrance | South exterior wall, x 3.65–4.55 at y 8.26. The model ends at the apartment boundary. |

These discrepancies follow the existing structural geometry. Wall coordinates and window openings are retained from v0.5. The courtyard is a fictional shared exterior model, with opposing plaster façades, residential windows and two visible directions.

## Rooms and furnishings

- **West bedroom.** Bottle-green 2.20 m sofa bed with broad arms, three seats and backs, and approximately 1.60 m open depth. OLED-style television and low cabinet opposite, desk under the window, wardrobe, rug, plants, floor and desk lamps.
- **East bedroom.** Bed and muted blanket, bedside cabinet and warm lamp, monitor desk and task light, bookshelves with accent light, plants, framed art and everyday objects. Yrliet is a fictional graphic designer who works from home.
- **Kitchen.** Cream cabinetry, wood-effect worktop, small beige backsplash tiles, white refrigerator, gas stove, open shelves, wooden table and four mismatched chairs. Flowers, plants, mugs and dishes add everyday detail. The pendant and warm countertop fixture operate independently.
- **Bathroom and utility.** Original working layout with water fixtures and laundry storage, plus towels and toiletries.

Tall painted windows with deep sills and ribbed radiators, painted panel doors with brass-colored handles, procedural herringbone and lightly textured plaster establish the period character. The 3D objects are lightweight spatial references for composition.
